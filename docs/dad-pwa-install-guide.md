# StoreResolve on your iPhone — setup

Takes about 5 minutes. A few simple steps to get StoreResolve on your Home
Screen with notifications turned on.

**Before you start:** your iPhone needs to be reasonably up to date. Check
Settings → General → About → "Software Version" — it should say 16.4 or
newer. (If you're not sure, just try the steps below; they'll tell you.)

1. On your iPhone, open **Safari** (the blue compass app) and go to:
   **store-resolve.moogzie.workers.dev**
2. You'll see a login screen. Type in your email: **ajvohra1@yahoo.com**.
   It will email you a one-time code — open your Yahoo mail and type the
   code in. (Last time the email took a few minutes to arrive, so give it
   a moment; check your spam folder if you don't see it.)
3. Once you're logged in, tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow
   pointing up, at the bottom of the screen), scroll down, tap
   **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**. You now have a StoreResolve icon
   on your Home Screen.
4. Open StoreResolve from the new **Home Screen icon** (not from Safari).
   If it asks you to log in again, that's normal the first time — just do
   step 2 once more.
5. Inside the app, open **Settings**, tap **Push notifications**, then
   **Enable**. When the iPhone asks if you want to allow notifications,
   tap **Allow**.
6. The page should now say **"Enabled on this device"** — that means
   you're all set.
7. **Text Manav "done."** He'll send one test notification himself so you
   know it's working. Nothing will come through before that.

**If something looks off:**
- Code email never arrived → wait a few minutes, then check spam. Yahoo
  was slow last time too.
- No "Add to Home Screen" option → make sure you're in Safari, not Chrome
  or another browser.
- The installed app asks you to log in again → normal the first time; log
  in once more and it sticks.
